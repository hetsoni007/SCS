Why scoping matters

Most founders get their app wrong because they didn't scope it right.

❌

Scope creep — You add features mid-build because you didn't think through what's actually MVP and what's Phase 2. Timeline doubles, costs triple.

❌

Wrong tech choice — You pick a stack based on what your friend used, not what your app needs. Six months in, you realise you chose the wrong framework.

❌

Bad vendor choice — You hire the cheapest team because you didn't know what questions to ask. They build you something that can't scale, and you're stuck rewriting.

This guide teaches you the exact questions and framework we use so you don't make these mistakes — and you know exactly what to expect from any team you work with.

Who this is for

Founders. Technical co-founders. Product leads.

If you're building a mobile or web app and want to understand how to scope it properly before you talk to any team, this guide is for you.

It's agnostic — you might end up working with us, or you might build in-house, or you might hire someone else. Either way, this guide will save you time and money.

The only people this won't help: if you've already built your app and it works perfectly, you probably don't need it.
