Field notes

201 real outreach threads

“No” and “nothing” are different answers.

Of my 201 outreach threads, 35 were explicit rejections: a real person typing “not interested.” Another 70 were silence. My CRM filed both under the same status and resurfaced them a week later. I nearly sent a third follow-up to someone who had asked me, in writing, to stop.

CRM status model

  • 35RejectedSaid no in words. Closed permanently and never resurfaces.
  • 70Gone quietSaid nothing. Slower list, different angle, months later.
  • 96The restThe remaining threads of the 201.
  • 105“Not responding”One status for both. Resurfaced every week, whatever they had said.
  • 96The restThe remaining threads of the 201.

If your CRM has one status for “not responding,” split it before you build anything else on top of it.

The follow-up I deleted

Typed, then deletedHey [name], just wanted to follow up — let me know if you’re still interested!

Read back from the prospect’s side, it said nothing. It could have gone to anyone, about anything, and it matched what forty other vendors had already sent him. I replaced it with one line built from something he had said three weeks earlier. He replied.
