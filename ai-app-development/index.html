How we ship AI

From idea to a smart
app in four moves.

01

Define the win & the eval

We start from the outcome — the manual step to kill or the metric to move — and write the test cases that prove the AI works before we build it.

02

Pick the right model & pattern

Claude or GPT, RAG vs fine-tune, on-device vs cloud, single call vs agent. We choose for accuracy, latency and cost — not hype.

03

Build into React Native + MERN

The AI ships inside a real product — clean UX, a MERN backend, vector store, caching and guardrails for when the model is wrong.

04

Measure, ship, improve

Instrumented from day one — cost, latency and quality visible. We launch behind a human-in-the-loop, then expand as the data earns trust.
