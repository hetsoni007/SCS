How the score works

The score adds three parts and clamps the total between 1 and 10. The weights are judgement calls drawn from the audit, not a statistical model, so here they are in full:

Signal
Stated pain+5
Just launched+5
Rebuild under way+4
Hiring post+2
Job title only, no stated intent+1
Company size
1–10 people+1
11–50 people+2
51–200 people+1
201–1,000 people0
1,000+ people−1
Days since the trigger
Day 0–7+3
Day 8–30+2
Day 31–90 (end of the window)+1
Day 91–180−2
Day 181+−3

The timing bands follow the 0–90 day window from signal-based targeting. A high score means “audit this site today”. A low one means find a better signal first, which is cheaper than writing a good email to the wrong person.
