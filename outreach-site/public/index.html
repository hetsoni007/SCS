A teardown of my own cold outreach

I sent 200+ cold emails and got zero replies.

Most people fix cold outreach by rewriting the email. I counted instead: 201 threads, zero positive replies, 35+ explicit rejections, and 50+ messages sent to people who were never going to buy. The email was never the main problem.

This is what the count showed, and the system I rebuilt from it. Written down, with the tools I use.
