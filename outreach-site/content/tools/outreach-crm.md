## The rules it enforces

- **Two touches, then it closes.** After touch 2, the only things you can log are a reply, a no, or closing the thread with no reply. There's no third-touch button: not a disabled one, not a hidden one. It doesn't exist.
- **Touch 2 has to carry something new.** You can't log it without saying what's new since touch 1, and "just following up" doesn't count.
- **A "no" is final.** Logging a rejection closes the thread, and the same person can't be added again to start over.
- **Auto-replies don't count.** You can log them, but they don't move the thread. The prospect is still waiting on a human.
- **Contacted prospects can't be deleted.** Otherwise deleting and re-adding someone would be a third touch with extra steps.

These come straight out of [the audit](/articles/the-audit/): {{rejections}} explicit rejections, some of which still got a third or fourth message because my tracking was scattered.

## Your data

Your list lives in this browser tab until you save or export it. Save and export ask for your email once. The list itself never leaves your browser.
