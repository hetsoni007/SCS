We build apps that close deals, not demos that impress meetings.

Soni Consultancy Services was founded on a simple frustration: too many agencies were great at winning pitches and slow at shipping products. We built the studio we wished existed — senior engineers, no junior handoffs, and a culture where the client's first week post-launch matters more than the proposal deck.

We've shipped products for healthcare, logistics, fintech, retail and creator platforms — many of them live on the App Store and Play Store today. Our clients are founders who need to move fast, CTOs who need to trust a partner, and CMOs who need AI to actually deliver ROI.

Our edge is being a boutique. You get senior people on every engagement, 48-hour kick-offs, daily updates in a shared Slack, and an honest answer when something's out of scope. We say no to retainer chasing, vague timelines, and junior developers on senior projects.

How we work

Built differently,
intentionally.

01
Outcome-first thinking

We ask what business success looks like before writing a line of code. Every technical decision traces back to a user or revenue outcome — never to engineering preference.

02
Founder-speed delivery

First working build in days. No 6-week discovery phases, no bloated agency retainers. We move like a startup because, operationally, we are one.

03
Radical transparency

Daily progress updates, shared Slack channel, honest timelines. You'll never send a "just checking in" message to us. If something changes, we tell you the same day.

We build with Claude API, OpenAI and LangChain where it genuinely helps — not as a trend, but as a real competitive edge for your product. We also know when to say no to AI.
