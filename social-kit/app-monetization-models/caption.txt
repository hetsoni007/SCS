"How will your app make money?" deserves better than a shrug. 💸

The four models that cover almost every app 👇

🔁 Subscription — recurring value, recurring revenue. Depth + retention beat raw reach.
🧩 Freemium + IAP — free core, paid depth. The craft is the split.
📺 Ads — impressions × eCPM. The math is honest: no big engaged scale, no money.
🤝 Commission — a cut of the transactions you enable. Scales with volume, needs liquidity.

Two rules: follow your usage shape, and prove ONE model before layering. And model the store cut BEFORE you set prices.
