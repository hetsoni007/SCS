Native or cross-platform? Pick wrong and your app can cost 2× more. 💸

Every founder hits this fork. Here's the honest version 👇

📱 React Native — one codebase → iOS + Android. Fastest, most cost-effective for most apps.
🎨 Flutter — pixel-perfect, fully custom UI from a single codebase.
⚙️ Native (Swift/Kotlin) — max performance & hardware access, at roughly double the build + maintenance.

The rule we give founders: start cross-platform. Go native only when a real requirement forces it — AR, heavy graphics, deep hardware.
