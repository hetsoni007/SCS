How Much Does It Cost to Build an AI App in 2026?

Every founder asks it and every agency answers "it depends." Here is the actual breakdown — the price bands, what moves them, and the running costs nobody quotes you up front.

Het Soni10 min read · May 2026

The honest answer

A production AI app in 2026 ranges from roughly £15k to £150k+ to build. That spread is real, not evasive — the difference between the low and high end is almost entirely scope and integration surface, not the AI itself.

What actually drives the cost

  • Integration surface. Every external system — CRM, payments, internal databases — adds build and test time. The biggest driver.
  • Data work. If the AI needs your data (retrieval, embeddings), cleaning and structuring it is often half the project.
  • Accuracy bar. "Good enough to assist a human" is far cheaper than "trusted to act unattended."
  • Compliance. Healthcare, finance and anything touching personal data adds audit trails and review cycles.

Three realistic tiers

Tier 1 — Focused MVP · £15k–£35k

One core AI feature, one or two integrations, a clean UI, human-in-the-loop. Built in 4–6 weeks. The right starting point for almost everyone.

Tier 2 — Growth product · £35k–£80k

Multiple AI features, real auth and roles, several integrations, an evaluation harness and analytics. 8–14 weeks.

Tier 3 — Scale platform · £80k–£150k+

Multi-tenant, mobile + web, agentic workflows, strict reliability and compliance, dedicated infra. 4–6 months.

The running cost nobody quotes: model usage scales with traffic. A busy chat feature can run from tens to thousands of pounds a month depending on context size and model choice. We design for this with caching and retrieval so it stays predictable.

The hidden costs

  1. Evaluation. You can't improve what you don't measure. Budget for an eval set — not optional.
  2. Prompt & model iteration. The first version is rarely the shipped version.
  3. Guardrails. Handling the cases where the model is wrong is real engineering.
  4. Maintenance. Budget 15–20% of build cost annually.

How to control scope

Start with the single feature that proves the thesis. Ship it behind a human review step. Measure. Only then expand. The teams that blow their budget try to build Tier 3 before validating Tier 1.

The cheapest AI app is the one you scoped down to its single most valuable feature and shipped in six weeks.
